TRAIN RUN PERFORM

Four week hybrid cycle. Three runs each week. Trap bar and single leg work keep the legs strong for race prep.

WEEK 1

Rebuild positions. Bring volume back online. Total per day 50 to 70 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

Trap bar is primary. Lean legs and strong single leg work stay in the mix.

A

TRAP BAR DEADLIFT

5 x 5 at RPE 7. Rest 2 to 3 minutes. Brace hard and stand tall every rep.

B

STEP UP

3 x 8 per side. Box around knee height. Full foot on the box. Control down.

C

LEG PRESS

3 x 12 moderate. Feet shoulder width. Push through mid foot and heel.

D

HAMSTRING AND CORE

Hamstring curl 12. Dead bug 10 per side. Three rounds. Rest 45 seconds.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS

Touch pace near ten K without going all out.

A

WARM UP

10 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

6 x 2 minutes at RPE 7 with 2 minutes easy jog between.

C

COOL DOWN

8 to 10 minutes easy.

DAY 3 UPPER STRENGTH

Pressing strength and simple pulling volume.

A

BENCH PRESS

5 x 5 at 70 to 75 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

SEATED STRICT PRESS

4 x 6 at RPE 7. Rest 90 seconds.

C

ROW SUPERSET

4 rounds. Chest supported row 10. DB incline press 10. Rest 75 seconds.

D

ARMS

Curl 12. Rope pressdown 12. Three rounds. Rest 45 to 60 seconds.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO

Bring back steady work around ten K feel.

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Four strides.

B

MAIN

2 x 12 minutes at RPE 6 to 7 with 2 minutes easy jog between.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy jog. Walk 2 minutes.

DAY 5 HYBRID ENGINE

Support the run with controlled engine work.

A

EMOM 20

Minute 1 bike 14 cals. Minute 2 KB swing 18. Minute 3 box step over 14. Minute 4 plank 45 seconds.

B

ACCESSORY

Nordic or assisted hamstring curl 8 slow. Copenhagen plank 20 seconds per side. Three rounds.

DAY 6 LONG RUN

Time on feet with relaxed pace.

A

MAIN

Easy run 60 minutes at RPE 3 to 4. Short walk minute every 15 if you like.

B

STRIDES

6 x 20 seconds fast but smooth with 60 seconds walk.

Load plan: if trap bar and bench feel clean, nudge weight up next week. If not, keep load and add one rep on the last set.

WEEK 2

Progress strength and threshold. Total per day 55 to 75 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

Trap bar load climbs. Single leg stays honest.

A

TRAP BAR DEADLIFT

6 x 4 at RPE 7 to 8. Rest 2 to 3 minutes.

B

FRONT FOOT ELEVATED SPLIT SQUAT

3 x 8 per side. Slow down for two seconds. Rest 90 seconds.

C

LEG PRESS

4 x 10. Last two reps feel strong but not grindy.

D

CALVES AND CORE

Standing calf raise 15. Side plank 25 seconds per side. Three rounds.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS

Work near tempo and half marathon feel.

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

4 x 6 minutes at RPE 7 with 2 minutes easy jog.

C

COOL DOWN

8 to 10 minutes easy.

DAY 3 UPPER STRENGTH

A

BENCH PRESS

6 x 4 at 75 to 80 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

STRICT PRESS

5 x 5 at RPE 7 to 8.

C

ROW SUPERSET

4 rounds. Single arm row 10 per side. DB flat press 10. Rest 75 seconds.

D

CHEST FINISH

DB fly 12 slow. Push up 15. Two rounds.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO

Unbroken tempo for steady work.

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Four strides.

B

MAIN

30 minutes at RPE 6 to 7. Even pacing. Last five minutes feel strong, not frantic.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy jog.

DAY 5 HYBRID ENGINE

A

AMRAP 18

Row 12 cals. KB swing 16. Box step over 12. Keep RPE around 7. Move smooth, no sprints.

B

CORE

Pallof press 12 per side. Plank 40 seconds. Three rounds.

DAY 6 LONG RUN

Build toward marathon prep with calm volume.

A

MAIN

Easy run 70 minutes at RPE 3 to 4. Take some fuel once if you cross 60 minutes.

Load plan: if trap bar and bench feel solid, bump weight three to five percent next week. If fatigue is high, keep loads and pull one set off instead.

WEEK 3

Peak for this block. Strong lower work and sharper running. Total per day 60 to 80 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER HEAVY

Heaviest trap bar of the block. Keep quality first.

A

TRAP BAR DEADLIFT

7 x 3 at RPE 8. Rest 2 to 3 minutes. Leave one rep in the tank on every set.

B

REAR FOOT ELEVATED SPLIT SQUAT

3 x 8 per side. Use a load that feels strong but stable.

C

STEP UP

3 x 10 per side. Same box height each week so you can feel the progress.

D

LEG PRESS

3 x 10. Smooth tempo, no bouncing at the bottom.

DAY 2 RUN VO2

Shorter work closer to five K feel.

A

WARM UP

15 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

5 x 3 minutes at RPE 8 with 2 minutes easy jog. Pacing should be strong but repeatable.

C

COOL DOWN

10 minutes easy.

DAY 3 UPPER HEAVY

A

BENCH PRESS

7 x 3 at 80 to 85 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

PUSH PRESS

5 x 4 at RPE 8.

C

PULL SUPERSET

4 rounds. Weighted chin up 5. Chest supported row 12. Rest 75 seconds.

D

ARM TRI SET

3 rounds. Incline curl 12. Rope pressdown 12. Lateral raise 15. Rest 60 seconds.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO CHOICE

Pick the version that fits how you feel.

A

OPTION A

Continuous tempo 35 minutes at RPE 6 to 7.

B

OPTION B

3 x 12 minutes at RPE 7 with 2 minutes easy between.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy run. Walk 2 minutes.

DAY 5 HYBRID CAPACITY

Simple mixed piece that supports engine strength.

A

FOR TIME CAP 20

Row 750. DB snatch alt 20 total. Box step over 20. Row 500. Snatch 16. Step over 16. Row 250. Snatch 12. Step over 12. Keep RPE near 8 but keep movements clean.

DAY 6 LONG RUN EXTENDED

Longest run in this block to keep marathon prep moving.

A

MAIN

Easy run 80 minutes at RPE 3 to 4. Take fuel around 35 to 40 minutes and again if needed.

Peak note: this is the highest load week. If trap bar or bench speed slows hard, drop the last set and keep quality over volume.
