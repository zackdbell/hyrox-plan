Week 1

Build base. Reintroduce volume. Three runs this week.

Day 1 Strength Priority

  • Warm up 5 to 8 min easy cardio then hip and shoulder prep
  • Back Squat 4 x 5 at 70 to 75 percent or RPE 7
  • Romanian Deadlift 3 x 8 at RPE 7 then Rest 60 to 90 sec
  • Superset 3 rounds: DB Bench 10 reps and Chest Supported Row 12 reps
  • Accessory circuit 2 rounds: Split Squat 10 per side and Hollow Hold 30 sec

Long 55 to 65 add one set. Short 30 to 40 use 3 x 5 squat. Home swap Goblet Squat and DB RDL.

Day 2 Run Intervals

  • Warm up 8 to 10 min easy then 4 strides
  • 6 x 400 m at RPE 7 to 8 with 200 m jog or 90 sec walk
  • Cool down easy jog 5 to 10 min

Total 30 to 45. Target 5K pace. Treadmill or outdoor.

Day 3 Strength Priority

  • Warm up 5 to 8 min then band shoulder series
  • Deadlift 4 x 4 at 72 to 78 percent or RPE 7
  • Strict Press 4 x 6 at RPE 7
  • Superset 3 rounds: Walking Lunge 12 per side and Hanging Knee Raise 12
  • Finisher 8 min AMRAP: 10 Cal Row and 10 KB Swing

Long 55 to 65 full volume. Short 30 to 40 cut one set. Home swap DB Deadlift and DB Push Press.

Day 4 Run Tempo

  • Warm up 10 min easy then 4 strides
  • Tempo run 20 min at RPE 6
  • Cool down 5 to 8 min easy

Total 35 to 45. Hold 10K effort.

Day 5 Conditioning (Optional)

  • Warm up 5 min bike
  • EMOM 20: Thrusters, Step Overs, Cal Bike, Plank

Long 55 to 65 add EMOM 10. Short 30 to 40 EMOM 16. Home swap step ups and bodyweight squats.

Day 6 Easy Long Run (Optional)

  • Warm up 5 to 10 min
  • Run 45 to 60 min at RPE 3 to 4
  • Add strides every 10 min

Total 45 to 65. Walk run 4:1 if needed.

Progression notes: Add 5–10% load or volume next week.

Week 2

Build strength and aerobic support. Three runs again.

Progression notes: Small load bump. Longer run work.

Week 3

Peak week. Higher quality work. Hold form.

Progression notes: Highest load week. Next week deload.

Week 4

Deload week at eighty to ninety percent volume. Keep rhythm.

Progression notes: Restart next block with retests and load bumps.
