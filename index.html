TRAIN RUN PERFORM

Four week hybrid cycle. Three runs each week. Percentages are one rep max based or use RPE.

WEEK 1

Base build. Quality volume. Total per day 50 to 70 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

Goal force and position. Long track 65 to 70. Short track 45 to 55.

A

BACK SQUAT

5 sets x 5 reps at 70 to 75 percent. Rest 2 to 3 minutes.

B

RDL

4 sets x 8 reps at RPE 7. Rest 90 seconds.

C

SPLIT SQUAT + ROW

3 rounds. Split Squat 10 per side. Chest supported row 12. Rest 75 seconds.

D

CALVES + CORE

3 rounds. Standing calf raise 15. Dead bug 12 per side. Rest 45 seconds.

E

MOBILITY

8 minutes hips and ankles. Breathe nasal only.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS

Total 45 to 60. Work near five K pace.

A

WARM UP

10 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

8 x 400 meters at RPE 7 to 8. Recover 200 meter jog or 75 to 90 seconds walk.

C

AEROBIC ADD

10 minutes easy jog if time allows.

D

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy. Light hips.

DAY 3 UPPER STRENGTH

Push and pull balance. Total 55 to 70.

A

BENCH PRESS

5 x 5 at 72 to 77 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

STRICT PRESS

4 x 6 at RPE 7. Rest 90 seconds.

C

PULL UP SUPERSET

4 rounds. Pull up 6 to 8. DB incline press 10. Rest 75 seconds.

D

ARMS FINISH

3 rounds. Cable row 12. EZ curl 12. Rope pressdown 12. Rest 60 seconds.

E

BREATH WORK

3 minutes box breathing. Four count each side.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO

Total 45 to 55. Ten K effort.

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Four strides.

B

MAIN

Tempo 25 minutes at RPE 6 to 7. Even pacing.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy run. Walk 2 minutes.

DAY 5 HYBRID ENGINE

Mixed modal conditioning. Total 45 to 60.

A

EMOM 20

Minute 1 wall ball 20. Minute 2 bike cals 14 men or 10 women. Minute 3 box step over 14. Minute 4 plank 60 seconds.

B

RECOVER

Easy spin 6 minutes.

C

ACCESSORY

Hamstring curl 12. Copenhagen plank 20 seconds per side for 3 rounds.

DAY 6 LONG RUN

Aerobic base. Total 55 to 70.

A

MAIN

Easy run 55 to 60 minutes at RPE 3 to 4. Walk one minute every 10 if needed.

B

STRIDES

6 x 20 seconds relaxed with 60 seconds walk between.

C

POST

Mobility 8 minutes. Calves and hips.

Load plan: add five percent next week on main lifts if bar speed holds. If not, add one rep per set.

WEEK 2

Progress volume and speed. Total per day 55 to 75 minutes.

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

A

BACK SQUAT

6 x 4 at 75 to 80 percent. Rest 2 to 3 minutes.

PAUSED RDL

C

LUNGE COMPLEX

3 rounds. Walking lunge 12 per side. GHD or back extension 15. Rest 60 to 75 seconds.

D

CORE

Hanging knee raise 12. Pallof press 12 per side for 3 rounds.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

5 x 800 meters at RPE 7 to 8. Recover 400 meter jog or 2 minutes walk.

C

AEROBIC ADD

10 minutes easy run after if legs feel good.

D

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy.

DAY 3 UPPER STRENGTH

A

BENCH PRESS

6 x 4 at 77 to 82 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

STRICT PRESS

5 x 5 at RPE 7 to 8.

C

PULL SUPERSET

4 rounds. Weighted pull up 5 to 6. Single arm row 10 per side. Rest 75 seconds.

D

CHEST FINISH

DB fly 12 slow. Push up 15. Two rounds only.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Four strides.

B

MAIN

Tempo 30 minutes at RPE 6 to 7. Hold even pace.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy jog.

DAY 5 HYBRID ENGINE

A

AMRAP 18

12 KB swing 53 or 35. 12 burpee. Run 300 meters or row 15 cal. Keep RPE 7.

B

ACCESSORY

Rear foot split squat 12 per side. Ring row 15. Three rounds.

C

BREATH RESET

5 minutes nasal easy spin.

DAY 6 LONG RUN

A

MAIN

Easy run 60 to 65 minutes at RPE 3 to 4.

B

STRIDES

6 x 20 seconds with 60 seconds walk.

C

POST

Mobility 10 minutes. Feet and hips.

Load plan: move five percent up on squat and bench if bar path stays clean. Deadlift adds one set next week.

HIP THRUST

ACCESSORY GIANT SET

MOBILITY

MAIN

AEROBIC ADD

D

COOL DOWN

PUSH PRESS

C

ARM TRI SET

OPTION A

OPTION B

A

FOR TIME CAP 20

ACCESSORY

DAY 6 LONG RUN EXTENDED

A

MAIN

STRIDES

8 x 20 seconds with 60 seconds walk.

C

POST
