TRAIN RUN PERFORM

Four week hybrid cycle. Three runs each week. Strength supports the engine. Percentages are one rep max based or use RPE.

WEEK 1

Rebase and skill. Total per day 50 to 70 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

Intent clean positions and moderate load. Long track 65 to 70. Short track 45 to 55.

A

FRONT SQUAT

5 x 5 at 68 to 72 percent. Rest 2 to 3 minutes.

B

ROMANIAN DEADLIFT

4 x 8 at RPE 7. Two second down. One second hold mid shin.

C

UNILATERAL

Rear foot split squat 10 per side. Superset with chest supported row 12. Three rounds. Rest 75 seconds.

D

CALVES AND CORE

Standing calf raise 15. Dead bug 12 per side. Three rounds. Rest 45 seconds.

E

MOBILITY

Hips and ankles 8 minutes. Nasal breathing only.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS HILLS

Total 45 to 60. Strengthen stride and economy.

A

WARM UP

10 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

10 x 60 second hill at RPE 7 to 8. Jog down recovery.

C

FLAT STRIDES

4 x 20 second fast and relaxed. Walk 60 seconds.

D

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy. Light hips.

DAY 3 UPPER STRENGTH

Push pull balance. Total 55 to 70.

A

BENCH PRESS

5 x 5 at 70 to 75 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

SEATED STRICT PRESS

4 x 6 at RPE 7. Rest 90 seconds.

C

PULL SUPERSET

4 rounds. Pull up 6 to 8. DB incline press 10. Rest 75 seconds.

D

ARMS FINISH

3 rounds. Cable row 12. EZ curl 12. Rope pressdown 12. Rest 60 seconds.

E

BREATH WORK

Box breathing 3 minutes. Four count each side.

DAY 4 RUN TEMPO

Total 45 to 55. Ten K effort feel.

A

WARM UP

12 minutes easy. Four strides.

B

MAIN

2 x 12 minutes at RPE 6 to 7 with 2 minutes easy between.

C

COOL DOWN

8 minutes easy run. Walk 2 minutes.

DAY 5 HYBRID ENGINE

Mixed modal aerobic. Total 45 to 60.

A

EMOM 20

Min 1 wall ball 20. Min 2 bike cals 14 men or 10 women. Min 3 box step over 14. Min 4 plank 60 seconds.

B

RECOVER

Easy spin 6 minutes.

C

ACCESSORY

Hamstring curl 12. Copenhagen plank 20 seconds per side for 3 rounds.

DAY 6 LONG RUN

Aerobic base. Total 55 to 70.

A

MAIN

Easy run 55 to 60 minutes at RPE 3 to 4. Walk one minute every 10 if needed.

B

STRIDES

6 x 20 seconds relaxed with 60 seconds walk.

C

POST

Mobility 8 minutes. Calves and hips.

Load plan: next week add five percent on main lifts if bar speed holds. If not, add one rep per set.

MAIN
