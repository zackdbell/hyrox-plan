Train Run Perform

Four week hybrid plan. Strength first. Three runs each week.

Week 1

Build base. Reintroduce volume.

Day 1 Strength Lower

Long 55 to 65. Short 30 to 40. Home swaps listed.

A

Primary

Back Squat 4 x 5 at 70 to 75 percent or RPE 7

B

Superset

Romanian Deadlift 3 x 8 and DB Bench 10

C

Accessory

Split Squat 10 per side and Chest Supported Row 12 for 2 rounds

D

Finish

Hollow Hold 30 sec and Calf Raise 15 for 2 rounds

Short track use 3 x 5 on squat. Home swap Goblet Squat and DB RDL.

Main Set

C

D

Cool Down

5 to 10 min easy
