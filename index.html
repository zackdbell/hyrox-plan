WEEK 5

New cycle. Trap bar focus. Total per day 50 to 70 minutes.
›

DAY 1 LOWER STRENGTH

Trap bar is main lift. Single leg work stays in for lean legs and control.

A

TRAP BAR DEADLIFT

5 x 5 at RPE 7. Rest 2 to 3 minutes. Clean lockout.

B

FRONT FOOT ELEVATED SPLIT SQUAT

3 x 8 per side. Slow down for two seconds. Rest 90 seconds.

C

STEP UP

3 x 10 per side. Drive the knee and stand tall. Rest 75 seconds.

D

CORE

Dead bug 12 per side. Side plank 25 seconds per side. Three rounds.

DAY 2 RUN INTERVALS

Rebuild speed without max effort.

A

WARM UP

10 minutes easy. Drills. Four strides.

B

MAIN

8 x 90 seconds at RPE 7. Recover 2 minutes easy jog.

C

COOL DOWN

8 to 10 minutes easy.

Pressing strength with solid pull volume.

BENCH PRESS

5 x 5 at 70 to 75 percent. Rest 2 minutes.

B

SEATED STRICT PRESS

4 x 6 at RPE 7.

C

ROW SUPERSET

4 rounds. Chest supported row 10. DB incline press 10. Rest 75 seconds.

D

ARMS

Curl 12. Rope pressdown 12. Three rounds. Rest 45 seconds.

Support running without beating the legs up.

A

EMOM 20

Min 1 bike 14 cals. Min 2 KB swing 18. Min 3 box step over 14. Min 4 plank 45 seconds.

B

ACCESSORY

Hamstring curl 12. Copenhagen plank 20 seconds per side. Three rounds.

MAIN
